Expected policy path EFFR after each meeting

Method

Each meeting changes the policy rate on the decision date plus the effective lag (normally 1 day). Daily EFFR fixings follow the Fed holiday calendar and daily SOFR fixings follow the SIFMA calendar (Good Friday included). Weekends and holidays carry the prior business day's fixing. Fixings before the pricing date are rebuilt backward from the current EFFR.

ZQ = 100 − calendar-day average EFFR over the contract month. SR1 = 100 − calendar-day average SOFR. SR3 = 100 − compounded SOFR from the 3rd Wednesday of the contract month to the 3rd Wednesday three months later (Act/360, business-day compounding). SOFR = EFFR + the spread, plus the turn on the last business day of each month (the quarter-end and year-end values replace the month-end one).

Outrights, and custom baskets whose weights do not net to zero (packs), are shown as an average price. Spreads, flies and condors are Σ weight × price, shown in bp. Δ = fair B − fair A. Edge = fair B − market. A positive edge means the market is cheap, so the signal is to buy.

Implying moves solves one meeting at a time against ZQ or SR1 prices under schedule A. When a meeting falls in the last week of its month and the next month has no meeting, the next month's contract is used because it pins the post-meeting rate more cleanly.
